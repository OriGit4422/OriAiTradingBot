Critical Risk Rule:
The bot must risk only 2% of wallet balance per trade.
Risk must be calculated from entry price to stop-loss price.
Do not calculate position size from margin alone.
Do not place any trade without a stop-loss.
Do not place any trade if the calculated risk is above 2%.
Do not use full wallet balance as margin.
Do not use unrestricted maximum leverage.

Position Size Formula:
riskAmount = walletBalance * 0.02
stopDistance = abs(entryPrice - stopLossPrice)
positionSize = riskAmount / stopDistance
notionalValue = positionSize * entryPrice
requiredMargin = notionalValue / leverage

Example:
Wallet balance = $100
Risk amount = $2
Entry = 100
Stop-loss = 98
Stop distance = 2
Position size = 2 / 2 = 1 unit
Notional value = 1 * 100 = $100
At 5x leverage, required margin = $20
If required margin is too high, reduce size or block the trade.
